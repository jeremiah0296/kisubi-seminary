FORMING YOUNG MEN IN FAITH, KNOWLEDGE AND SERVICE.

Background

Kisubi Minor Seminary was founded in 1951 by Archbishop Joseph Louis Cabana and the Missionaries of Africa (White Fathers) to nurture young men for the Catholic priesthood.

Location

It is located at Kisubi along the Entebbe Road in Wakiso District, Uganda. It is primarily a place of academic, spiritual, moral and human formation for young men discerning a possible vocation to the priesthood

Why you should chose kisubi

The seminary combines: Academic education, Spiritual formation, Moral formation, Human and social development, Physical development. Preparation and discernment for priestly vocation.

Note

Importantly, not every student who attends Kisubi becomes a priest. The seminary states that many students eventually follow other careers, while benefiting from the academic, spiritual, moral and human formation they receive
