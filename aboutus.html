academic excellence, moral integrity, and spiritual growth through holistic
formation.

VISION

Transforming lives through faith and education by providing
a nurturing environment for spiritual and academic growth

Background

Kisubi Minor Seminary was founded in 1951 by Archbishop Joseph Louis Cabana and the Missionaries of Africa (White Fathers) to nurture young men for the Catholic priesthood.

Foundation and Vision

Core Goal: The school provides spiritual guidance alongside academic education for secondary students

Growth and Legacy

Holistic Training: The program focuses on four areas: spiritual growth, social development, human formation, and robust academics. Notable Alumni: Over 700 former students have become Catholic priests and bishops, while thousands more became leaders in education, governance, and public service. Platinum Jubilee: The institution celebrated its 75th anniversary on June 27, 2026.
